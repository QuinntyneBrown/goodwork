Process step

A process step is a card naming one step: its number, name, a one-line summary and the practices it holds. Steps sit in an ordered list.

Overview

Use for a short sequence of steps, two to six. The brochure uses four for the FaithTech Playbook 2.0 cycle.

  1. Step 1

    Lament

    We listen to the people affected before we reach for a solution.

    Listen, remember, repent, rejoice

  2. Step 2

    Discern

    We seek wisdom before choosing a response.

    Reject, receive, reimagine or create

Two steps of the work process.

Anatomy

  1. Number: overline, gold, "Step N".
  2. Name: h3 in the heading face.
  3. Summary: one sentence.
  4. Practices: a short muted list in prose.

Variants

One variant.

Sizes

One size; steps in a row share the tallest height.

States

Static.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 768px: one column.
  • 768px to 1023px: two per row.
  • 1024px and wider: four per row.

Theming

Uses the card surface; the number uses the gold foreground.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each step is a list item in an <ol>, so the order is announced.

Keyboard

KeyAction
NoneStatic.

Motion

None.

Touch

None.

Content

  • Name: one word.
  • Summary: one sentence starting "We".
  • Practices: three or four words, comma separated.

Do and don't

  • Do say when a process repeats, below the list.
  • Don't make a step a link.
