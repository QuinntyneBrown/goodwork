Process step

Process steps show a repeated process as numbered cards: a gold step number, a serif title and one sentence.

Overview

Numbered cards for the practices of a repeated process. On the brochure: the four FaithTech Playbook 2.0 practices under "How we work". They sit in an ordered list so screen readers announce the count and order.

Anatomy

  1. NumberOverline, gold, "Step n".
  2. TitleSerif h4.
  3. BodyBody small, muted.

Variants

  1. Step 1

    Lament

    We listen before we build.

  2. Step 2

    Discern

    We seek the wisest response.

  3. Step 3

    Build

    We build with open hands.

  4. Step 4

    Steward

    We care for what we release.
