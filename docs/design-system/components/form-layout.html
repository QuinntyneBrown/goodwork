Form layout

Form layout composes fields into a form: error summary on top, fields in one column, actions at the bottom with a hint beside them.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableFieldset, summary, actions

Overview

The contact form is the reference. One column. Error summary first when there are errors. Actions left aligned on marketing pages, with a short reassurance beside the button.

Anatomy

  1. Error summaryLinks to each field.
  2. FieldsSingle column.
  3. ActionsPrimary first on the left, hint beside it.

Variants

We reply within two business days.
Marketing actions (left, with hint) and app actions (right aligned, primary last).

One size.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Everything else uses the real attribute.

  • Actions wrap; the hint drops under the button below 400px.
