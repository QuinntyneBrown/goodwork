Card

Cards lift a group of content onto Paper: the service cards and the contact panel are both cards.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSurface

Overview

Use to group content that belongs together. Do not nest cards.

Anatomy

  1. HeaderTitle in serif h4.
  2. BodyAny content; 24px padding.

HTML
<div class="card" style="width: 100%; max-width: 14rem;"><p>Default</p></div><div class="card card--interactive" style="width: 100%; max-width: 14rem;"><a href="#variants">Interactive</a></div><div class="card card--selected" style="width: 100%; max-width: 14rem;"><p>Selected</p></div>

Sizes

One padding: <code>--space-6</code>. Use <code>.card--flush</code> for edge-to-edge media.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Everything else uses the real attribute.
