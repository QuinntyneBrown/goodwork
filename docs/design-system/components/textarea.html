Textarea

The contact form's message box. Help text sets expectations so the person does not feel they owe us an essay.

Overview

Use when the answer is more than one line. Pair with help text that says how much to write.

Anatomy

  1. LabelA question is fine here.
  2. TextareaMin 2.5× control height, vertical resize.
  3. HelpLinked with aria-describedby.

Variants

Who you serve, what you need, and roughly when.

52 of 1,000 characters

With help text and a counter.
HTML
<div class="field" style="width: 100%;"><label class="field__label" for="ta1">What are you working on? <span class="optional">(required)</span></label><textarea class="textarea" id="ta1" rows="4" aria-describedby="ta1-h ta1-c">We run a transitional housing program in Toronto.</textarea><p class="field__help" id="ta1-h">Who you serve, what you need, and roughly when.</p><p class="field__counter" id="ta1-c">52 of 1,000 characters</p></div>

Sizes

One size; height follows <code>rows</code>, minimum 2.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Everything else uses the real attribute.

Responsive behaviour
