Disclosure

A disclosure shows one question and hides its answer until the visitor asks for it. Stacked, disclosures make an FAQ.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: new, pages/home/defaultNative details/summary

Overview

Use for frequently asked questions and other optional detail. Do not hide anything a visitor needs to complete a task.

How does pricing work?

After a first conversation, which costs nothing, we send a fixed-price quote for the agreed scope.

Can we update the website ourselves?

Yes. We build websites you can edit yourself, and we show your team how.

Two disclosures in an FAQ list, the second open.
HTML
<div class="faq"><details class="disclosure"><summary class="disclosure__summary">How does pricing work?</summary><p class="disclosure__answer">After a first conversation, which costs nothing, we send a fixed-price quote for the agreed scope.</p></details><details class="disclosure" open><summary class="disclosure__summary">Can we update the website ourselves?</summary><p class="disclosure__answer">Yes. We build websites you can edit yourself, and we show your team how.</p></details></div>

Anatomy

  1. Summary: the question, semibold, with a gold plus or minus sign at the end.
  2. Answer: body text, muted.
  3. Rule: a hairline under each item; the FAQ list adds one above the first.

Variants

One variant.

Sizes

One size. The summary is at least 48px tall.

States

Closed (the default), hover, focus and open.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width up to the reading measure.
  • Long questions wrap; the sign stays on the first line's right.

Theming

Reads role tokens only; the sign uses the gold foreground, which is reversed in dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <details> and <summary>: the browser exposes the summary as a button that reports expanded or collapsed. No script.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the next summary.
Enter or SpaceOpens or closes the answer.

Focus

The standard focus ring on the summary.

Labelling

The question is the accessible name. The sign is CSS content, so it is not announced as a word.

Contrast

Answer on canvas
Sign on canvas

Motion

None.

Touch

The whole summary row is the target.

Content

  • Write the summary as the visitor's question, ending in a question mark.
  • Answer in one to three sentences. Lead with the answer.
  • No price figures.

Do and don't

  • Do keep every disclosure closed on load.
  • Don't put a heading or a link inside the summary.
