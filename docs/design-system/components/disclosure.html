Disclosure

Disclosures show and hide an answer under a question. Stacked, they make the FAQ.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative details

Overview

A native <details> and <summary>: the question is the control and the answer shows below it. Stacked disclosures form the FAQ list. Each opens on its own; opening one does not close the others.

Anatomy

  1. SummaryBody, semibold. The control.
  2. ChevronGold; points up when open.
  3. AnswerBody, muted.

Variants

How much will our project cost?

After a short call we send a fixed-price quote.

Why do you cost less than an agency?

A small senior team and an AI-assisted process.

FAQ list, one open.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width up to the prose measure (65ch). Summary is at least 44px tall at every size.

Theming

Default text on the canvas, gold chevron, default border dividers.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native disclosure; the browser exposes the summary as a button with its expanded state.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the next summary.
Enter / SpaceOpens or closes the answer.

Focus

Focus ring on the summary.

Labelling

The question text names the control. The chevron is CSS-only and decorative.

Motion

None; the chevron flips without animation.

Content

  • Summary is a question a client asks, in their words. Answer leads with the direct answer, in two sentences or fewer.
  • Never hide information needed to act, such as how to get in touch, only in a disclosure.
