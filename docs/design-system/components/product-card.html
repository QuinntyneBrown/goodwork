Product card

A product card names one product the studio offers, with an optional status badge.

Overview

Use for one product the studio offers. Until a product ships, one card with the "Coming soon" status holds its place.

Coming soon

Ready-made tools for ministries

We're building ready-made tools for churches, ministries and non-profits. Ask us about early access when you get in touch.

The placeholder card.
HTML
<div class="card product"><span class="badge">Coming soon</span><h3 class="product__title">Ready-made tools for ministries</h3><p class="product__body">We're building ready-made tools for churches, ministries and non-profits. Ask us about early access when you get in touch.</p></div>

Anatomy

  1. Status: an optional neutral badge, left aligned.
  2. Title: h3 in the heading face.
  3. Body: one or two sentences, muted.

Variants

With or without a status.

Sizes

One size; cards in a row share the tallest height.

States

Static.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width up to 40rem.

Theming

Uses the card surface and the neutral badge.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A card with an h3 under the section h2. The status is text, so it is read aloud.

Keyboard

None; static.

Contrast

Body on surface

Content

  • No price figures.
  • No link until there is somewhere real to go.

Do and don't

  • Do mark unreleased products "Coming soon".
  • Don't invent a product name for a placeholder.
