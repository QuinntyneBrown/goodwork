Menu

A menu offers several related actions from one trigger, so a card or row does not need a row of buttons.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: core, unused in mocksAPG Menu Button

Overview

Use a menu for three or more secondary actions on one object. Use buttons when there are one or two. Never put navigation in a menu that a person needs to find.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerA button with aria-haspopup and aria-expanded.
  2. ItemIcon plus verb.
  3. Danger itemLast, after a divider.

Variants

Checkable items and a disabled item.

Sizes

Menus have one size; item height is <code>--control-height-sm</code>.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Everything else uses the real attribute.

VariantDefaultHoverDisabled
Item

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

  • Menus open below their trigger and flip above near the bottom edge.
  • Under 640px, menus of more than six items become a bottom sheet (see dialog).

Theming

Menus use --color-bg-surface-raised and --shadow-3, so in dark they sit one step lighter than cards.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA APG Menu Button: role="menu" and menuitem.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter, Space, ↓Opens and focuses the first item.
↑ ↓Move between items.
Home EndFirst and last item.
EscCloses and returns focus to the trigger.
LettersType-ahead to an item.

Focus

Focus moves into the menu on open and back to the trigger on close.

Labelling

The trigger names the object; items are verbs.

Contrast

Motion

Scales in from the trigger over --duration-fast.

Touch

Items are 32px tall inside a menu that is itself the target area.

Content

  • Items are verbs in sentence case.
  • Destructive items go last, after a divider, and open a confirmation.
