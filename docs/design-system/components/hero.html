Hero

The hero says who Goodwork serves and why it costs less, in four short blocks.

Overview

Used once, at the top of the brochure. The eyebrow is the only gold text on the page.

Anatomy

  1. EyebrowOverline in --color-fg-gold.
  2. HeadlineSerif display, h1.
  3. LeadBody large, muted.

Variants

Software for people doing good work

Professional apps, at a price a small mission can afford.

Goodwork is a small software studio that builds websites and custom applications for non-profits, churches and ministries.

Why it costs less: a small, senior team and an AI-assisted process.

Sizes

Headline is h1 (36px) on phones and display (48px) from 768px.

Professional apps

HTML

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Everything else uses the real attribute.
