Video player

The browser's own player in a 16:9 frame: a poster until pressed, captions on, never autoplay.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableMarketing

Overview

Used once, beside the hero, for the twenty-second pitch. It is the native <video> element: no custom chrome, so every browser's keyboard, caption and full-screen behaviour comes for free.

Anatomy

  1. Frame16:9, --radius-lg, subtle background while the poster loads.
  2. PosterThe video's title slide, 960×540.
  3. Native controlsPlay, scrub, volume, captions, full screen.

Variants

Default: poster shown, paused.
HTML
<figure class="video"><video class="video__media" controls preload="metadata" playsinline poster="../../videos/01-pitch/01-pitch-poster.jpg" aria-label="Goodwork pitch"><source src="../../videos/01-pitch/01-pitch.mp4" type="video/mp4"><track kind="captions" src="../../videos/01-pitch/01-pitch.vtt" srclang="en" label="English"></video></figure>

Sizes

One size: the frame fills its container's width and keeps 16:9.

States

  • Paused (default): poster and controls.
  • Playing: captions burned in and the VTT track on.
  • Focus: the page focus ring around the frame.
  • Ended: the browser shows replay.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 1024px: full content width between the services and the contact form.
  • 1024px and wider: the right column, top-aligned beside the hero copy, above the contact form (.brochure__video).

Theming

Frame background and border follow --color-bg-subtle and --color-border-default; the poster itself is light in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <video controls> with an aria-label. No autoplay (WCAG 1.4.2, 2.2.2); captions track (1.2.2); the narration speaks every word on screen, so no separate audio description is needed (1.2.5).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the player, then its controls.
Space / EnterPlay or pause.
ArrowsSeek and volume, per browser.

Focus

Page focus ring on the frame.

Labelling

aria-label="Goodwork pitch" on the element; the track is labelled "English".

Contrast

Burned-in captions are white on a dark grey plate; the native controls are the browser's.

Motion

None until play is pressed, under any motion preference.

Touch

playsinline keeps playback in the page on phones; native targets.

Content

  • The script lives in docs/videos/01-pitch/script.md and repeats page copy, never new claims.
  • Keep the clip under thirty seconds.
