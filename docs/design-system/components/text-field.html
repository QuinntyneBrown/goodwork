Text field

Text fields take a short answer. The contact form's email field is the reference: visible label, required in words, inline error.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <input>

Overview

Use for one line: email, name, organisation. Use a textarea for anything longer. Always wrap it in a form field.

Anatomy

  1. LabelAbove the input, required stated in words.
  2. Input40px tall, strong border, Paper background.

Sizes

Medium is the default. Large matches a large submit button.

Heights from --control-height-*.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Everything else uses the real attribute.

  • Read-only is how the sending mock freezes input: the value stays readable and copyable, but cannot change.
  • Invalid pairs a red border with an icon and text; colour alone never signals the error.

Responsive behaviour

  • Inputs are full width of their column at every breakpoint.
  • Font size never drops below 16px, so iOS does not zoom on focus.

Theming

Inputs sit on --color-bg-surface with a --color-border-strong edge; dark uses the Sage edge on ink-700.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <input> with <label for>; aria-invalid and aria-describedby for errors.

Keyboard

Gold ring at 0 offset on the input edge.

Labelling

Visible label always; placeholders are hints only. Use autocomplete="email" and inputmode.

Content

  • Labels are nouns: Email, not Enter your email.
  • Mark required in words: (required).
  • Errors say how to fix it: Enter a complete email address like name@organization.org.
