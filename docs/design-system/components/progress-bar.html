Progress bar

Progress bars show tasks longer than two seconds.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: core, unused in mocksrole="progressbar"

Overview

Not on the brochure. Use for uploads and imports.

Anatomy

  1. FillAccent.
  2. TrackSubtle hover tint.

Variants

Determinate, indeterminate, success.
HTML
<div class="progress" style="--value: 60%;" role="progressbar" aria-valuenow="60" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" aria-label="Uploading brief"><div class="progress__bar"></div></div><div class="progress progress--indeterminate" role="progressbar" aria-label="Importing contacts"><div class="progress__bar"></div></div><div class="progress progress--success" style="--value: 100%;" role="progressbar" aria-valuenow="100" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" aria-label="Done"><div class="progress__bar"></div></div>

Sizes

One height: 8px.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Everything else uses the real attribute.

VariantDeterminate
Bar

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width.

Theming

Fill is the accent; Linen in dark.
