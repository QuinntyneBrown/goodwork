Foundation

Color

Deep evergreen for trust, linen for warmth, and one note of gold. Every pairing below is checked against WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Four brand colours come from the Goodwork brand sheet: Evergreen Ink, Cornerstone Gold, Linen and Paper. Stone and Line support them. Gold appears once per view as an accent: the cornerstone block, the hero eyebrow, the service icons. It is never a button or a large fill.

Usage

  • Text is Ink on Linen or Paper. Secondary text is Stone.
  • Primary actions are Ink with Linen labels; in dark they reverse to Linen with Ink labels.
  • Gold text uses --color-fg-gold (gold-700 in light, gold-300 in dark), never raw Cornerstone Gold, which is under 4.5:1 on Linen.
  • Status colours keep the starter hues; they are functional and sit apart from the brand.
