Content

Overview

Goodwork's voice is warm but understated. We talk to people doing good work, not at them. Faith is named plainly once, never used as a sales line.

Voice and tone

  • Plain words over jargon: apps, not digital solutions.
  • Concrete jobs: volunteer scheduling, case management.
  • Reassure about cost and effort: A sentence or two is plenty.

Rules

  • Sentence case everywhere except the name Goodwork.
  • Buttons are verbs: Send request, Start a project, Try again.
  • Dates are human: Thu 23 Oct 2026. Times say the zone when it matters.
  • No exclamation marks in errors.

Formulas

  • Error: what happened, that their work is safe, what to do now. We couldn't send your request. Your message is still here. Try again, or email us.
  • Success: what happened, what happens next, whether they need to do anything. Request sent. We'll reply within two business days. There's nothing else to do right now.
  • Empty: what is empty, why, the one next step.
