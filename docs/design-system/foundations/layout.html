Foundation

Layout

Mobile first, four breakpoints, a 4/8/12 column grid and a 1280px container.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • Grid
  • Page templates
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

The brochure is one column on phones and tablets and two columns from 1024px: story and services on the left, the contact card on the right. At 1280px wide the whole page fits in 1043px of height, under the 1100px limit set for it.

Page templates

  • Brochure: top bar, container, two-column grid at 1024px, contained footer.
  • App shell: top bar plus sidebar at 1024px.
  • Error page: top bar plus centred empty state.

Usage

  • Never scroll the page sideways; tables scroll in their wrapper.
  • Prose stops at 65ch.
