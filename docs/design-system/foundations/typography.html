Overview

A classic, bookish serif carries the voice; a plain sans carries the work. Headings h1 to h4, the display size and the wordmark are serif. Body, labels, buttons and inputs are sans. Both are self-hosted in production; the mocks fall back to Georgia and the system sans.

Usage

  • Body is 16px and never smaller in inputs.
  • Nothing below 12px.
  • Headings use text-wrap: balance; paragraphs use pretty and stop at --layout-measure.
  • The hero headline is h1 size on phones and display size from 768px.

Accessibility

  • Sizes are in rem so text scales with browser zoom.
  • Line height is at least 1.5 for body text.
  • Overline text is uppercase with 0.04em tracking and only used for short labels.
