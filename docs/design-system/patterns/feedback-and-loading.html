Pattern

Feedback & loading

Every action gets a visible response within 100ms, in the place the person is looking.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

The brochure gives feedback inline: the busy button, then a success or error alert where the form was.

When to use

  • Inline alert: the outcome belongs to a region (the contact form).
  • Toast: a background action in an app.
  • Skeleton: content loading.
  • Progress bar: over two seconds with a known end.

Structure

Request sent. Thank you, Ruth.

Busy, then confirmed.
HTML
<button class="btn btn--primary btn--lg" type="button" aria-busy="true">Sending…</button><div class="alert alert--success" role="status"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true" viewBox="0 0 24 24"><path d="m5 12 5 5L20 7"/></svg><div class="alert__body"><p class="alert__title">Request sent. Thank you, Ruth.</p></div></div>

States

  • Under 300ms: show nothing.
  • Over 300ms: busy state on the button pressed.
  • Done: say what happened and what is next.

Responsive

  • Feedback stays next to the control at every width.

Accessibility

  • Success in role="status", errors in role="alert".
  • Busy buttons set aria-busy.

Content

  • Past tense for done, present progressive for busy: Sending….
