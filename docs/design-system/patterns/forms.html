Pattern

Forms

The contact form is the model: two fields, one button, five states.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

  • When to use
  • Structure
  • States
  • Responsive
  • Accessibility

Overview

Ask for as little as possible. The brochure asks for an email and a few lines, nothing else.

When to use

  • Use an inline form when the page exists to collect the answer.
  • Use a dialog form only for a quick edit inside an app.

Structure

A sentence or two is plenty.

We reply within two business days.
Single column, labels above, help under, actions at the end.
HTML
<form class="contact__form" novalidate aria-label="Contact example" style="width: 100%;"><div class="field"><label class="field__label" for="px-email">Email <span class="optional">(required)</span></label><input class="input" id="px-email" type="email" autocomplete="email"></div><div class="field"><label class="field__label" for="px-msg">What are you working on? <span class="optional">(required)</span></label><textarea class="textarea" id="px-msg" rows="3" aria-describedby="px-msg-h"></textarea><p class="field__help" id="px-msg-h">A sentence or two is plenty.</p></div><div class="form-actions"><button class="btn btn--primary btn--lg" type="button">Send request</button><span class="contact__hint">We reply within two business days.</span></div></form>

States

  • Default: empty, no errors.
  • Invalid: summary on top, inline errors, focus to summary. Validate on submit, then live per field.
  • Sending: button busy, fields read-only, never cleared.
  • Sent: form replaced by a success alert that says what happens next.
  • Error: input kept, danger alert with retry and an email fallback.

Responsive

  • One column at every width.
  • The hint under the button on phones.

Accessibility

  • novalidate with custom messages.
  • aria-invalid and aria-describedby per field.
  • Focus to summary, success or error alert after submit.

Content

  • Labels ask human questions.
  • Required said in words.
  • Errors start with the fix.
