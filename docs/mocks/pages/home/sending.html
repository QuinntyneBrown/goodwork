How we work

We follow the FaithTech Playbook 2.0, a rhythm for building technology redemptively. It repeats for the life of your product.

  1. Step 1

    Lament

    We listen before we build, sitting with the people your work serves until we understand what is broken.

  2. Step 2

    Discern

    We seek the wisest response, which is not always new software: reject, receive what exists, reimagine it, or create.

  3. Step 3

    Build

    We build in short cycles of prayer, prototype and pause, ready to change course when the work drifts.

  4. Step 4

    Steward

    We take responsibility for what we release: we tend it, examine its fruit and tell its story truthfully.

Frequently asked questions

How much will our project cost?

It depends on what you need. After a short call we send a fixed-price quote, so you know the full cost before any work begins.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. Your quote comes with a timeline, so you know when to expect each milestone before any work begins.

Do you only work with churches and ministries?

No. We work with non-profits, churches, ministries and companies whose work makes life better for someone.

Can we update our website ourselves?

Yes. We build sites your team can edit without calling us, and we show you how.

What happens after launch?

We stay with you. Stewardship is part of our process: we fix what breaks, listen to the people using it and help you decide what comes next.
