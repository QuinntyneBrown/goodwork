# L1 - High-Level Requirements

## L1-001: Brochure home page
The system shall present a single public home page that describes Goodwork, explains why its work costs less than an agency's, states that it is a Christian-owned studio, and lists the services it offers: web and mobile apps, and websites. Below the contact form it introduces Goodwork's products, explains its work process (the FaithTech Playbook 2.0) and answers frequently asked questions.

## L1-002: Contact request
A visitor shall be able to send Goodwork a project enquiry from the home page by giving an email address and a short message, and shall be told clearly whether the request was sent, what is wrong with it, or that it failed and how to reach Goodwork another way.

## L1-003: Request delivery
Every accepted contact request shall be stored durably and delivered to Goodwork's inbox so that someone can reply within two business days.

## L1-004: Security and abuse protection
The public site and its contact endpoint shall resist abuse and attack: input is validated on the server, spam and floods are limited, and visitors' data is protected in transit and at rest.

## L1-005: Responsive layout and theming
The home page shall work on every viewport from extra small to extra large, and in light and dark themes.

## L1-006: Accessibility
The home page shall meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

## L1-007: Performance
The home page shall load quickly on a mobile connection and the contact endpoint shall respond promptly.
