# Goodwork demo narration

Voice: en-US-AvaMultilingualNeural, rate +8%, pitch +3Hz. Each paragraph is one caption and one spoken clip, in capture order. Headings are `## id — Chapter title`.

## opening — Welcome

Hi, and welcome to Goodwork! We're a small software studio that builds websites and apps for people doing good work. Let's take a quick tour of the site, one journey at a time.

## meet — Meet Goodwork

This is the Goodwork home page. At a glance you can see who we are: a small studio building websites and custom applications for non-profits, churches, ministries, and companies whose work makes life better for someone.

Cost matters, so we explain it plainly. A small, senior team, an AI-assisted process, and a fixed-price quote before any work begins. We're also a Christian-owned studio, glad to serve anyone whose work serves others.

Scroll down and you'll find the two things we build: web and mobile apps, and fast, accessible websites that you can edit yourself.

## theme — Light and dark

Prefer a darker look? The site follows your device. Switch to dark mode and the whole page changes at once, with the same careful contrast. And back to light again, with no reload.

## phone — On a phone

It works just as well on a phone. The layout stacks into a single column, buttons stay easy to tap, and nothing scrolls sideways. Your visitors on the go are covered.

## keyboard — Keyboard only

Not everyone uses a mouse, so everything works from the keyboard. Press Tab and a skip link appears, with a clear focus ring. Press Enter and you jump straight past the header to the content. Keep tabbing, and you reach the contact form in a sensible order.

## start — Start a project

Ready to talk? Choose Start a project. The page glides to the contact form and puts your cursor in the email field, so you can start typing right away.

## mistakes — Fixing mistakes

We keep the form short: just an email and a few lines about your project. If something's missing, we tell you clearly. Send it empty, and a summary appears and takes focus, naming exactly what to fix.

Choose a problem in the summary and you land in that field. Type a complete email address and its error disappears as you go, with no need to send again.

## sending — Sending a request

Now let's send a real one. Add an email address and tell us about your project. On a slow connection the button shows that it's sending, and your words stay right where they are.

And there it is: request sent. We confirm the address we'll reply to, and promise an answer within two business days.

## another — Sending another

You can send another request any time. But to keep spam away, each visitor is limited to five requests every ten minutes. We sent four test requests before this video began, so this one will be the sixth.

## spam — Protection from spam

The form tells you it couldn't send, keeps your message exactly as you typed it, and offers the studio's email address so you can write directly. Nothing is lost, and nobody is left guessing.

## studio — It reaches the studio

So what happened to our first request? It was saved safely, and a background job emailed it to the studio inbox. If the mail server is busy, it keeps trying again, a little later each time, so nothing gets lost.

Here's the message that arrived. The sender's address is set as the reply-to, so the studio can answer with a single click.

## closing — Thank you

That's Goodwork: clear, fast, accessible, and secure, built for people doing good work. If you have a project in mind, we would love to hear about it. Thanks for watching!
